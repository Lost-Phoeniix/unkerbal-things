# Unkerbal Things

Two things in the Kerbol system that should not be there.

- **The Moho creature.** Something lives in the ground of Moho and Eve: thousands of worms that move as one. It cannot see you. It feels the ground: touchdowns, engines firing close to the surface, wheels, drills, impacts. It travels under the surface, and when it finds you it comes up from below. A craft high enough, or fast enough, gets away. One that is caught does not.
- **The Laythe leviathan.** Deep in Laythe's sea (below 400 m) a 45 m fish circles underneath you, comes up to look at you from close by, and in the end rises from far below at full speed. It only destroys a craft it reaches fast enough, and never in the first three minutes.

The presence of either one is felt before it is seen: the screen degrades like a failing video feed, the hull creaks, the music fades, instruments glitch. Close to the Moho creature the outside view fails altogether; only a camera on the craft, or a kerbal's own eyes, still show anything.

## Parts

Six parts for studying the Moho creature, in the science and utility tabs:

| Part | Tech node | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Swarm Spotter | Electronics | Rough bearing and distance to whatever moves under the ground. |
| Ground Proximity Radar | Electronics | Height and terrain ahead, with a warning beep, for flying without an outside view. |
| Geophone Array | Scientific Tech | Seismic recordings on the ground. |
| Heavy Proximity Scanner | Advanced Science Tech | Scans the creature from close by. |
| Swarm Imager | Advanced Exploration | A heavy camera head for the top of a craft: its own feed (right mouse aims, wheel zooms), photographs and energy recordings from the air. |
| Seismic Signal Generator | Experimental Science | Strikes the ground. Something may answer. |

## Install

Copy `GameData/UnkerbalThings` into your KSP `GameData` folder. KSP 1.12. The screen effect and the Moho creature's body need Direct3D 11.
BDArmory Continued is optional: with it the creatures show up as targets and can be shot.

## Settings

`GameData/UnkerbalThings/settings.cfg`, every option explained in the file. `debugTools = true` adds a toolbar button with spawn buttons and other testing tools.
