# Legacy: the original monsters (reference only, not built)

A copy of the whole source as it was before the mod was cut down to the Moho creature and the Laythe leviathan and renamed.
It still has the original mod's other monsters and features: Mimic and the ground entities (scientist, tourist), the Rake, the Laythe land creature,
the Eve sea monster, the Jool eye, the Grabber, the Dres anomaly, the approaching "box" signal with its map contacts and countdown, the night ambience,
the random horror music and the encounter science.

Nothing here is compiled (`Compile Remove="legacy\**"` in the project). Their models, sounds and pictures are not in this repository: they came with
the original release, mostly from Sketchfab, Pixabay and Voicemod, and several are not cleanly redistributable.
Bringing a monster back means new, licensed assets and, best, its AI rebuilt in the style of the leviathan
and Moho (pure brain in Dread/, game side in its own EntityTracker.<Name>.cs).
